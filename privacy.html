Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

LocalCut provides a free tool for removing backgrounds from images directly in your web browser. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or use our website.

1. How image processing works

LocalCut processes selected images locally in your web browser, using your device's processing resources. Images selected for background removal are not uploaded to LocalCut's servers for processing.

The website downloads scripts, AI models and other resources from external providers to make the tool work, including jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and IMG.LY's model hosting (staticimgly.com). When your browser fetches these files, those providers can see technical information such as your IP address and browser type. These resources are separate from the image you select. LocalCut does not send your selected image to these providers as part of its normal background-removal process.

Your processed image is made available in your browser so that you can download it to your device. LocalCut does not operate an account system for saving your images online.

2. Information processed when you visit

You do not need to create an account or provide personal details to use the background-removal tool.

However, when you visit a website, technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and request times may be processed by the website's hosting provider and other services involved in delivering the site.

The information actually collected depends on the services in use and their configurations.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

LocalCut may display advertisements using Google AdSense. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information such as IP addresses, cookie identifiers, browser information and interactions with advertisements.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent invalid activity and, where permitted, personalise advertising. Advertising-related data processing is separate from LocalCut's local image-processing function.

You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, a consent message is shown before advertising cookies or similar technologies are used for personalised advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the privacy settings link or message provided on the site, where available. You can also manage personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings or opt out of non-personalised tracking through your browser settings.

4. Google Analytics

LocalCut uses Google Analytics to count visitors and understand which pages are used. Analytics may process technical information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, browser and device type, and, where permitted, a cookie identifier. Your selected images are never sent to Google Analytics.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, analytics cookies and similar technologies are only used if you consent through the consent message. Before you choose, Google Analytics may receive limited signals that do not use analytics cookies on your device, such as that a page was loaded, which may include your IP address, browser type and the page visited.

In other regions, analytics may run by default. You can opt out by changing your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Cookies and browser storage

LocalCut uses browser local storage to remember your chosen colour theme, where supported by your browser. This preference is stored on your device.

Google or other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, security, analytics or service delivery, depending on which services are enabled and your consent choices.

6. Why information may be processed

Information may be processed where necessary to:

  • Deliver and maintain the website.
  • Load the scripts and models needed by the image tool.
  • Remember your selected colour theme.
  • Protect the website and prevent abuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where enabled.
  • Count visitors and understand how the site is used, where permitted.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability.

7. Third-party services

LocalCut may rely on third-party hosting, libraries, model providers, analytics and advertising services. Those services may process technical information when your browser connects to them and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Although LocalCut is designed to process images locally, this does not mean that no information about your website visit is ever transmitted to other services.

8. Data retention and security

LocalCut does not intentionally store selected images on its own image-processing servers. Image data may remain temporarily in your browser while you use the tool.

Technical information handled by hosting providers or third-party services may be retained according to their practices and settings. No website or internet connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Under the UK GDPR and, where it applies to you, the EU GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent you have given, and make a complaint to the relevant data protection authority.

In the UK, you can find information about your rights at the Information Commissioner's Office website.

10. Children's privacy

LocalCut is a general-purpose image tool. We do not intentionally require users to provide personal information to process images. Any advertising and third-party services must be configured in accordance with applicable rules concerning children and privacy.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if LocalCut's features, technology or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.
