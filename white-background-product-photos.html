About LocalCut

LocalCut (sometimes written Local Cut) runs directly in your browser using your device's own processing power. Without server farms, we can keep it free with no subscription. Simple ad banners help cover our costs while your images stay on your device during processing.

How to get a white background on a product photo

  1. Choose your product photo (JPG, PNG or WEBP).
  2. Wait a few seconds while the background is removed.
  3. Click Background colour and pick the white swatch.
  4. Click Download PNG.

Marketplaces such as Amazon ask for a plain white main image, and a clean white background also keeps shop listings looking consistent on Etsy, eBay and Shopify. Shooting against a plain surface in even light gives the cleanest cutout.

Product photo questions

Is the white background pure white?

The white swatch fills the background with #ffffff, which is pure white. Shadows from the original photo are removed with the background, so add a soft shadow in an editor if you want one.

Will it work on shiny or transparent products?

Glass, chrome and very fine edges can come out rougher. Check the result at full size before you upload it to a listing.

Can I process lots of product photos?

Yes. There is no daily limit or watermark, so you can do them one after another. Each image is 25MB maximum.

Are my product photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The background is removed in your browser on your own device, so unreleased products stay private. See how it works.
